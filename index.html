About Hacktoberfest Hack Day

Bringing the global open-source celebration directly to Cerritos students and developers.

Hacktoberfest Hack Day Cerritos is a high-energy, single-day hackathon organized by the Whitney High School Computer Science Club (WHS CS Club). Every October, developers around the world come together for Hacktoberfest to contribute to open-source software. We are channeling that energy into an in-person, welcoming hack day on October 18th right here in Cerritos, California.

Whether you are writing your first line of Python, building a full-stack web application, or creating an interactive game, this event provides the perfect launchpad. We have eliminated rigid categorical tracks so you have 100% creative freedom to build what excites you—an open-source tool, a helpful community application, a game, or a creative tech project. The only constraint is that the project has to involve some element of an open source AI model (Ollama, Gemma, Qwen,)

With beginner-friendly workshops, dedicated student mentors,official Hacktoberfest swag, and an expo-style project demo session, our goal is to foster an inclusive community where everyone can learn, build, and share.
